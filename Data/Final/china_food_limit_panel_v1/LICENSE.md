## What is deliberately **not** in this package

**No standard is reproduced in full, or in substantial part.** We publish no
clauses, no test methods, no annex prose, no scanned pages, and no PDFs. Users
who need the standards' text must obtain them from the issuing bodies (the
National Health Commission and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs
publish them; CFSA hosts a retrieval platform).

The `raw_text` column in `quarantined_rows.csv` carries short diagnostic
fragments so that a reader can inspect why a candidate row failed an extraction
gate. `source_manifest.csv` records filenames, sizes, and hashes for source
identification. Users who redistribute or reuse source-derived labels or
fragments should assess the terms and legal rules applicable to their use.
