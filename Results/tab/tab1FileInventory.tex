\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Family-specific long tables}} \\
\path{pollutant_limits_long.csv} & 739 & Contaminant limits as printed, with the basis of expression, normalisation status, extraction path, and a normalised value only where conversion is justified. \\
\path{pesticide_limits_long.csv} & 38,024 & Pesticide maximum residue limits with cleaned and raw name strings, printed qualifiers, stable pesticide and food keys, review status, source page and coordinates, and separate edition and extraction source identifiers. \\
\path{mycotoxin_limits_long.csv} & 61 & Mycotoxin limits as printed, with a per-record confidence field. \\
\path{vetdrug_limits_long.csv} & 1,365 & Veterinary-drug limits with the printed target-animal cell alongside its canonical animal representation. The 2002 records carry Word-table locators. \\
\path{additive_limits_long.csv} & 6,148 & Additive maximum-use entries with CNS and INS identifiers, food-category codes and use-condition notes. \\
\addlinespace \multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Aligned category panels}} \\
\path{pollutant_category_panel.csv} & 260 & Contaminant values by version with per-version units, bases, source categories and cell status. \\
\path{pesticide_panel.csv} & 11,718 & Pesticide cells retaining every candidate value, unit, rule object and source record identifier, with stable keys and explicit status fields. \\
\path{mycotoxin_panel.csv} & 40 & Mycotoxin values by version at the aligned category level. \\
\path{vetdrug_panel.csv} & 559 & Veterinary-drug values by version at the aligned category level. \\
\path{additive_panel.csv} & 2,762 & Additive cells retained at the exact CNS and food-code level, with every candidate and rule object preserved. \\
\addlinespace \multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Member-level panels}} \\
\path{pollutant_member_panel.csv} & 390 & Contaminant members with the applicable host category by version and the category-restructuring flag. \\
\path{mycotoxin_member_panel.csv} & 55 & Mycotoxin members with the applicable host category by version and the category-restructuring flag. \\
\path{vetdrug_member_panel.csv} & 1,132 & Animal and tissue pairs expanded from the printed animal groups, with values and a trend for each pair. \\
\addlinespace \multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{English-name lookups}} \\
\path{entity_names_en.csv} & 753 & English substance names printed by the standards themselves. \\
\path{food_category_names_en.csv} & 394 & Food-category labels linked to published translations, with the match method, evidence and source document. Partial; additives and pesticides only. \\
\path{additive_names_en.csv} & 221 & Additive names linked to a published translation by CNS code. Partial. \\
\addlinespace \multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\emph{Metadata, audit and quarantine}} \\
\path{source_manifest.csv} & 25 & The source-identity key for the release. Covers 21 edition sources, two text-layer extraction copies and two identity-audit copies, each with its digest, role, release status and attributed row count. \\
\path{version_dates.csv} & 21 & Issue and effective dates for the coverage editions, with date provenance and the official-register cross-check. \\
\path{standard_version_timeline.csv} & 1,504 & The wider history of food-safety national standards as dated edition and amendment events. Ancillary to the five-family panel. \\
\path{standard_timeline_conflicts.csv} & 2 & Two ranked candidate rows for the one metadata conflict met in the wider-history build. \\
\path{pesticide_edition_reconciliation.csv} & 6 & Visible Chapter 4 rule counts and section rosters against each edition's declared aggregate, with printed qualifier counts. \\
\path{pesticide_ocr_crosscheck.csv} & 17,203 & Text-layer against OCR comparison groups for the two scanned editions, with matched values, one-sided rows, matching level and status. \\
\path{quarantined_rows.csv} & 12 & Fragments rejected by the extraction gates, with the rejection reason and source path. \\
\path{data_dictionary.csv} & 255 & Every released field, with type, nullability, allowed values, key role and a bilingual description. \\
